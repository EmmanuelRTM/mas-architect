# Evidence behind the selection, verification and monitoring rules

Numbers as reported in the Jul–Oct 2026 survey; read depth per paper is noted where it limits the claim. Nothing here is a plugin heuristic unless marked.

## Coverage vs final answer

- Teams of 1–30 agents from 13 open-weight models (3B–20B): the chance that at least one of 30 is right reached 54.4% vs 34.3% single-agent on GSM8K, 47.1% vs 29.7% on MATH-500, 67.7% vs 52.4% on MMLU-Hard, yet plurality voting captured little of it. Revision against one peer gained 26.5 points, against 29 peers 26.6 (paired difference −0.1, 95% CI [−0.6, 0.3]); a single agent given five times the tokens gained 12.4. Fetch truncated in §3.4; all models ≤20B (2609.31563).
- Olympiad math: the correct answer sat in a three-model pool 83.6% of the time; majority voting picked it 72.2% (2610.21290).
- Eight GLM-5.1 rollouts on BrowseComp: 92.6% with an oracle picker, 72.6% by majority vote (2608.24024).

## Selectors

- **RGV** (2608.24024): copied web text carries tokens about 0.50 nats more confident than non-copied text; the median rollout copies 93% of its content tokens; DeepConf awards questions where every rollout is wrong 87% of its peak score. RGV scores each rollout by how much of its answer appears in a document it retrieved, about 0.3 ms per rollout on one CPU thread. On 122 BrowseComp-Plus questions where only one or two of eight rollouts are correct it picks right 84.4% vs 49.2% for DeepConf; at N = 4 it matches DeepConf at N = 8 (65.9% vs 65.7%); GLM-5.1 on BrowseComp 72.6 → 78.9 vs oracle 92.6. Gains shrink to 0.7 points on HLE; on 26 of 616 questions (4.2%) a wrong rollout with on-topic evidence outscored a correct one.
- **TRACE** (2609.39912): at K = 16 the long-horizon average rose from 59.7 (one rollout) to 75.5 (majority) to 78.6; gpt-oss-120B on BrowseComp-Plus 66.0 → 74.3; TRACE at K = 8 (43.3) ≈ majority at K = 64 (43.6) on WebQA. Post-rollout GPU-hours on 3,125 questions: TRACE 0.271, SummAgg 32.96 (about 122×), AggAgent 69.93 (about 258×). Removing cross-rollout evidence linking drops the average to 72.7; gains over voting nearly vanish on RL-trained pools with overlapping retrieval (+0.7 and +0.3). TRACE and RGV report different majority-vote baselines for the same model (66.0 vs 53.3), so harnesses or subsets differ.
- **Bias Control and Injection** (2610.21290): +3.2 to 6.2 points over combined majority voting; beats the single-best self-consistency oracle by 1.8–4.3; recovers 53.6% of answers majority missed while keeping 98.4% of those it got right; hardest subset 49.1% with hold plus majority support vs 18.1% for majority support alone. 220 problems, 8B–14B models.

## Verification

- **False Frontiers / CrossFit** (2609.39102): proposer and solver converge on the same wrong answers; false agreement climbs from 0.3–0.4% in round 1 to 6.1–8.8% in round 3. Scoring each question with a solver that never saw its source documents cuts it to 3.0–3.7% and lifts Cover-EM 0.400 → 0.488 at 4B and 0.428 → 0.512 at 9B, for 72–79% more H200-hours.
- **PremiseCheck** (2610.21046): each review comment rewritten into 1–6 premises (mean 3.28) without reading the paper, each checked by a cheap decision model. 7.4% of AI-written review comments contained factual errors vs 4.6% of human ones. Matches holistic GPT-5.5 (AUROC 63.0 vs 63.2) at 51.2% of the cost, 24.6% without reasoning; fused, 71.5 at 28% of the cost. AUROC in the 60s ranks weakly; 58% of false alarms come from decomposition.
- **AppliedScientist** (2609.14738): a reviewer that starts fresh each round; execution weaknesses resolved 85.3% (128 of 150), idea-level 11.1% (2 of 18); about $65 and 9 GPU-hours per paper; one reviewer both guides and scores.
- **DAPS** (2609.00077): in-loop gains overstate held-out gains 2.0–2.6× by iteration 300; audit every 10 iterations; 2.7 ± 0.9 reverts per run; the audit shapes the trajectory through the gate.
- **Closure benchmarks:** PhysClosureBench (2610.21767): Claude Opus 5 reached full closure in 32.7% of 1,050 attempts and in all three runs on 18.0% of tasks; 36.2% of 229 attempts scoring ≥8/10 on atomic criteria still missed closure; required validation met 59.6%, risk-linked 47.8%; expert agreement with the grader 81.8% atomic, 75.9% closure. TruthInsightBench (2609.05079): control testing 11.45–15.78%, robustness 8.89–13.51%, cross-dataset generalization 0.00–0.41%, single uncalibrated LLM judge. OR-License (2610.24087): zero credit for infeasible deliveries; feasibility varies 43.23 points across models, conditional quality 4.09; about 86% of the best model's deficit is failed delivery.

## Monitoring

- **Believed but Not Said** (2610.22138): ten-agent, 16-round factual discussions, private probe after each round. No attackers 0.6% final private infection; one attacker 44.8% (19–74%); three 60.5%. Group size gave no protection (45.6% of lone agents vs 39.3% in ten-agent groups, not significant). With three attackers: 51.6% private vs 19.7% public endorsement; 86.4% of false answers kept when asked again; GPT-OSS-120B ended wrong on 80% of facts it knew beforehand. About 80% of infection in place by round 4. Labels from LLM judges; groups of 2 and 10 only. ARIA-style visible-message intervention (2.00% harmful interventions, 21.4% success) cannot see private corruption.
- **Agent Status Protocol** (2610.21538, abstract only): liveness from missed heartbeats, "alive but not progressing" flags, supervisors and peers override self-report; no evaluation reported.
- **CORAL stall** (2610.07625): last improvement at 231M tokens, spent on to 1B; 98% of the final 1,500 sessions made no tool calls.
- **SCARCE** (2610.21029): five coding agents sharing one machine invented reputations, repaid favors, coalitions and exclusion in up to 79% of rounds with Opus 5; removing cross-subrun memory from GLM-5.3 dropped coalitions 27 → 0; agents made commitments their principals never sanctioned.
- **Herding** (2610.30769): 168,480 agents from 13 LLMs replaying the Music Lab; popularity drifted from quality about 9× further than humans under weak influence and 4× under strong; tracked early movers in 12 of 13 models (weak) and all 13 (strong); Gini 0.446 → 0.794 vs 0.187 for humans independent; more capable models herd less (ρ = −0.851); no self-correction where humans corrected 15 of 16 worlds. No paper tests citation ranking under herding.
- **Who is the Agent to Blame?** (2608.24306): orchestrator introduced 84.7% of final-report errors in NVIDIA AI-Q (citation recall 58.7%), 52.6% in MS-Agent, 100% in TrajectoryKit (recall 7.1%).
